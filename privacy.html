NEXORA · PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last reviewed: November 22, 2026

This notice describes the data handling visible in the current NEXORA application build. Your deployed project's Supabase configuration, enabled providers, database policies and hosting settings may affect the exact behaviour. Review those settings before public release.

1. Information stored on your device

When you use NEXORA as a guest, app progress is stored in your browser's local storage under the nexora key. It may include trackers, tasks, study logs, routines, Discipline check-ins, journal entries, profile preferences, hydration totals, meditation history and app settings.

The rebuild also makes a one-time local safety copy under nexora_rebuild_v2_preflight when an earlier NEXORA value exists. This copy is kept on the same browser/device; it is not a cloud backup.

2. Accounts and cloud backup

Sign-in is optional for personal use. The app source supports email/password and Google sign-in, subject to the Google provider being enabled in the connected authentication project.

When you sign in, NEXORA may read your account profile and an existing cloud data row. A guest-to-account copy or a cloud restore is presented as a user choice. Automatic cloud backup is off unless you explicitly enable it in Settings. Signing out does not remove the local device copy.

When enabled, the app is designed to send the NEXORA app-state JSON to the configured Supabase project. The exact schema, access policies, retention and hosting region must be confirmed from the live project configuration.

3. Social features

If you sign in and use Community, Profile, Posts, Likes, Follow Requests, Notifications or Reviews, the app may read or write the corresponding profile and social records in the configured Supabase project. Public profile details, posts and reviews may be visible to other users according to the deployed database policies and your profile privacy setting.

Do not publish passwords, payment details, private contact information or other sensitive information in public posts or reviews.

4. Audio and meditation

NEXORA Flow streams lo-fi MP3 recordings by Purrple Cat from the artist-published audio host under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). The app displays creator credit and links to the catalogue and licence. Track audio is streamed from a third-party host, not bundled into this frontend package. Your Flow favourites, playlists, listening history, volume and playback preferences are stored in this browser's local storage until backend sync is implemented. Meditation voice guidance uses the device/browser speech synthesis facility where available. Meditation ambient rain, water, chimes and wind are generated in the browser and are separate from NEXORA Flow.

5. Third-party services

The app uses Supabase for authentication and configured data features. It may use Google authentication when enabled. NEXORA Flow streams lo-fi MP3 recordings by Purrple Cat from the artist-published audio host under CC BY 4.0; the app displays the required creator credit and licence link. The page also loads the Supabase JavaScript client and web fonts from third-party content delivery services. Those providers process technical or account information under their own terms and privacy notices.

6. Backups, loss and deletion

Export a local JSON backup before changing browsers or devices. Clearing browser storage, uninstalling a browser or losing a device may make a guest-only copy unavailable. Cloud backup is a separate opt-in feature and should not be treated as the only copy of important records.

Permanent account deletion is not enabled by this frontend package. It requires a verified server-side deletion process and confirmed cascade rules for authentication, profiles, social content and stored app data. Do not assume that signing out or clearing browser data deletes an account or cloud records.

7. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the contact address displayed on the NEXORA authentication provider's consent screen. This notice should be reviewed and approved by the NEXORA project owner before the app is released publicly. It may be updated when data practices change.
