Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Welcome to NEXORA. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the NEXORA application.

1. Information We Store

NEXORA stores information you enter, such as tasks, study logs, journal entries, routines and tracker data, in your browser's local storage on your device.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used to display and manage the features you use within NEXORA. The current version does not provide account-based cloud synchronisation.

3. Data Storage

Information stored in local storage remains on the browser and device where you use NEXORA. Clearing browser data, changing browsers or changing devices may remove or make this information unavailable. You are responsible for keeping your own backup of important information.

4. AI Features

The current version of NEXORA does not connect to an external AI service. Any assistant-style responses currently available are generated within the application.

5. Notifications

NEXORA may request browser notification permission. Notifications are subject to your browser and device settings. The current version does not provide guaranteed background or push notifications.

6. Third-Party Services

NEXORA is hosted using a third-party hosting provider. Your browser may communicate with that provider to load the application. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

NEXORA is a general productivity and study application. It does not knowingly collect personal information through a dedicated account system in the current version.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when NEXORA's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact email displayed on the NEXORA Google OAuth consent screen.
